How it
works.

Two panels slot together in seconds. Place the top, give it a quarter turn, and a fitted wooden latch on the underside locks into the uprights, pulling the whole structure tight. No tools, no hardware. Take the top off the same way, and everything folds flat into one neat stack, ready to carry.
